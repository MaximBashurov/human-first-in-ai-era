## About People Being Different

While one of the general questions in [Thought](../index.md) sounds like:

> It is not quite clear whether CEOs are visionaries or just morons blinded by their boundless greed ...

...it is necessary to point at the prejudice risk: we can always make general conclusions on categories, including the categories of people, but specific personalities within **any** category can be **very different** from the general pattern, and people, moreover, "tend" to differ from each other.

For example, in the interview below, Demis Hassabis, the Co-Founder & CEO of Google DeepMind, sounds and behaves as an extremely intelligent person, being aware and giving a balanced view to most of the questions asked and researched by these Docs.
