# Testing

What does actual testing (usage) of current AI tell about its impact on Humans, including our understanding of its capabilities?

## Some Cases

| Result Nature | Case | Usage Nature |
|----------|----------|----------|
| Success | Tesla FSD | From user perspective - super clear usage. |
| Success | Gemini and analogues for general knowledge extraction | Highly [nuanced](../testing/experience-and-usage-results/mb-personal-experience-with-ai.md#nuances-of-ai-usage-chat-data-extraction) compared to traditional Search. |

| Success | Google’s flood forecasting | 7-day advanced warnings to 700 million people across 100 countries. |
