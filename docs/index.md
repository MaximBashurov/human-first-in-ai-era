<div class="grid cards" markdown>

-   **Thought** [:octicons-arrow-right-24:](../thought/)

    ---

    It is frighteningly unclear which **choices** would be good enough to survive in AI Era. But if for at least a moment we cope our fears, it becomes clear that some of our choices are not related to survival, but rather to **what we are** and what we **choose to consider important**. These Docs ask 17 questions that are the result of choice of this type: a choice to **consider Humans more important than any technologies**.

    ---

-   **IO** [:octicons-arrow-right-24:](../io/)

    ---

    **Information Overload (IO):** Whenever you go online with your idea, project or content, you find that "that guy" (1K of "guys", or worse - a number of cool teams, companies, corporations) have already produced an enormous amount of content of this type, spent years on that, have a huge audience of 100K+ attentive followers. This completely applies to any AI-related topic in 2026.
