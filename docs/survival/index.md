## "Mirror-Parasite" vs. "Mirror-Symbiot"

As stated, these docs are "[Thinking in Progress](../thought/index.md#rules-of-consistency-and-carte-blanche-for-survival)". Here is an extremely important general thought arising from the current understanding of [AI capabilities](../thought/thought-per-question/question-14.md#current-conclusions-and-open-questions) and limits:

> AI is a **representation (Mirror) of healthy (big, [consuming](../thought/thought-per-question/question-12.md#full-economics-collapse-scenario)) Human Society**, it will [**degrade**](../thought/thought-per-question/question-14.md#data-knowledge-limits) if Society weakens or collapses.

AI is a Planetary Nervous System.

* It cannot exist without the **Body** (The Planet's [resources](../thought/thought-per-question/question-14.md#physical-recourse-limits)).
* It cannot exist without the **Blood** (The [Economy/Circulation](../thought/thought-per-question/question-12.md#full-economics-collapse-scenario)).
* It cannot exist without the **Soul** ([Human Novelty/30% Rule](../thought/thought-per-question/question-14.md#data-knowledge-limits)).

**Conclusion**: If we treat AI as a way to replace humanity, it will degrade and collapse. If we treat it as a way to amplify a healthy society, it might actually help us hurdle the physical limits of the planet.

**"Mirror-Parasite" vs. "Mirror-Symbiot"**

How AI is a "mirror":

* It is [trained on humanity knowledge](../thought/thought-per-question/question-14.md#understanding-ai-capabilities) and **reflects** it.
* It is dependent on human generated novelty and **reflects** its absence by degrading.
* It is dependent of human population and well being and **reflects** it either by increased intelligence or by degrading if population weakens or decreases.

> AI can be "Mirror-Parasite" or "Mirror-Symbiot" but this entirely depends on how Humans will behave - will they address "immediate harm" (fired people who cannot pay bills or find new job) or not.

Current analysis of how BigTech thinks about people and how the mass layoffs are addressed, highlight the **core immediate problem**:

!!! warning "Immediate Threat Is Not Addressed"

    **None** of CEOs, none of BigTech or LLM Companies, none of politicians, "experts", scientists or other commenters think about immediate threat to people survival. The **complete silence** is kept about bills that cannot be paid and lives being destroyed right now.

That definitely mean that current humankind's choice is the "Mirror-Parasite" branch maximally increasing the probability of "[1% of survivors](../thought/thought-per-question/question-12.md#alternative-all-resources-for-1-of-survivors)" or "[full economics collapse](../thought/thought-per-question/question-12.md#full-economics-collapse-scenario)" scenarios.
