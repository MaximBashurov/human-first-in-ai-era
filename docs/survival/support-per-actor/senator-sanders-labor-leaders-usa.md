# Senator Sanders, Labor Leaders (USA)

This page documents **support for workers**—legislative proposals, union positions, public campaigns, and coalition statements—in the United States where Senator Bernie Sanders and major U.S. labor leadership are visible actors on technology, layoffs, and corporate power.

It belongs under [Support per Actor](index.md) because the question here is not only ideology but **concrete** demands (severance, notice, bargaining rights, taxation, antitrust, sectoral transition funds, and similar) aimed at people harmed or at risk during the AI transition.

## Start of Movement

As of 16 April 2026, we see the starting movement around the urgent questions related to the impact of AI on people well being. This initial movement is visible in US and led by Senator Bernie Sanders.
