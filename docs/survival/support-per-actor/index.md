# Support per Actor

The [Survival](../index.md) section argues that **immediate harm** to people caught in the AI transition is often left unaddressed. This subsection collects material **by actor**—companies, labs, platforms, and other organizations—about what they actually offer (or omit) when their strategy, products, or layoffs affect livelihoods, safety, and dignity.

The contrast with [Thought per Actor](../../thought/thought-per-actor/index.md) is intentional: there we ask what actors **say** and **signal** about humans and AI; here we ask what **support** (compensation, transition programs, hiring commitments, public accountability, third-party verification, and so on) exists on the ground.
