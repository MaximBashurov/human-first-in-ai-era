The presented Oracle layoffs were a result of a complex intersection between technological shifts (AI) and geopolitical pressures (US Policy). However it is worth noting that the "domestic hiring" discussion at the Oval Office was specifically about bringing high-value AI and engineering roles back to the US, while simultaneously cutting legacy or higher-cost US roles that no longer fit the new AI-focused infrastructure.

## Resource Reallocation (AI, not "Domestic Hires")

The budget saved from these salaries was explicitly funneled into **Project Stargate**—Oracle's massive partnership with OpenAI and NVIDIA to build **$100 billion** AI superclusters.

> **Key Fact:** Oracle reported (March 2026) a **95% jump in net income** (**$6.13 billion**) last quarter. The layoffs are not due to "revenue distress," but are a calculated move to free up **$8–10 billion** in cash flow to fund a **$156 billion** AI data center build-out. 

## Impact Breakdown

The layoffs were part of a massive strategic pivot where Oracle redirected capital from traditional maintenance teams toward "AI expansion" and high-end compute hardware.

| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **India** | **~2,880 Employees** | Based on a 10% cut of a ~28,800 headcount in hubs like Bengaluru and Pune. |

## What happened to People Initially? (August 2025)

* **Severance Packages:** Most affected staff received approximately **15 days of salary per year of service**, along with extended medical benefits (up to 12 months in some regions).
* **Security Revocation:** Due to the sensitive nature of Cloud Infrastructure (OCI), many employees reported their system access was revoked within minutes of the notification email.

## April 2026 Context

As of 1 April 2026, the situation has escalated. While the August 2025 cuts were significant, they were the precursor to a much larger "second wave" currently unfolding this month. 

> **Note:** Current reports indicate that because the 2025 pivot did not fully offset the cost of AI infrastructure debt (approx. **$58 billion**), Oracle is now targeting a further **18% reduction** in global workforce, potentially impacting up to **30,000 additional roles**.

## What happened to People After 8 Months? (Layoff of August 2025)

Between August 2025 and the moment of creation if this article (April 2026), **8 months have passed**. An average employee with 6 years of service received 3 months of salary (90 days). Combined with the 2-month "top-up" often offered for amicable resignation, most had a financial cushion of 5 months.

* **Current Status**: Reports from April 2026 indicate that many of these former employees are still struggling to find "equally paying jobs" at short notice, leading to what experts call a "**personal tragedy**" for mid-career professionals.
* **The 2026 "Double Hit"**: Many who found contract work or roles in smaller firms are now being hit again. As of March 31, 2026, Oracle [initiated](#current-context-april-2026) a massive new wave of 30,000 layoffs (18% of their global workforce). This has flooded the job market with an additional 12,000 tech workers in India alone, making competition for the remaining roles extreme.


**The Financial "Cliff" (Month 8 Analysis)**

For those laid off in August 2025, the standard severance (approx. 3–5 months of runway) typically ran out by **January or February 2026**. Today, they face a reality where:

* **Savings Depletion:** Without new roles in an oversaturated market, many have exhausted emergency funds and are now dipping into retirement accounts (EPF/401k) or selling personal assets (gold, vehicles) to pay basic utilities.
* **The Stock Trap:** Oracle’s stock has seen extreme volatility, dropping nearly **48% in the last six months**. Employees who held "vested units" as a secondary safety net have seen half of that value vanish exactly when they needed it most.
* **The EMI/Mortgage Crisis:** In tech hubs like Bengaluru or Seattle, missing three consecutive mortgage payments (EMIs) has triggered legal recovery actions for many mid-career professionals.

**Survival Strategies in 2026**

When bills can no longer be met through traditional means, the "survival infrastructure" becomes informal and desperate:

* **The "Reverse Migration"**: In India, a massive trend of **reverse migration** has seen thousands of engineers vacating high-rent apartments in cities like Bengaluru and Pune to move back to parental homes in Tier-2/3 cities. This is often accompanied by the "prestige trauma" of returning home "defeated."
* **The Gig Economy Pivot**: Senior architects and managers are increasingly taking entry-level freelance roles in **"AI Data Labeling"** or **"Prompt Testing."** These roles pay roughly **10% of their former salaries** but provide just enough cash flow for groceries and medicine.
* **Debt Shuffling**: In the US, individuals are reportedly "cycling" credit card debt—using one card to pay the minimum of another. With interest rates remaining high, this has created a "ticking time bomb" for household solvency.
