# Support per Country

Institutions that can cushion an AI-driven labor and welfare shock—social insurance, retraining funds, sectoral bargaining, migration rules, competition and liability law—vary sharply **by country**. This subsection is meant to document that variation in one place: not abstract optimism about "the future of work," but **concrete** programs, gaps, and debates as they exist in each jurisdiction.

Use it together with [Layoffs and Survival Cases](../layoffs-and-survival-cases/index.md) for episodes that already span several geographies, and with [Support per Actor](../support-per-actor/index.md) when the same company behaves differently under different legal regimes.
