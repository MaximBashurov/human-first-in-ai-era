# USA

Federal law, agencies, and national unions interact with **state** labor codes, unemployment insurance, and sectoral conditions—so "the United States" is never a single uniform regime. This page collects **country-level** (primarily federal and cross-state) material on support for people affected by AI-driven labor and welfare shocks.

!!! note

    Prefer citations to statute, agency guidance, and primary statements. Add subsections (e.g. federal programs, major bills, regulatory dockets) as sources are added.
