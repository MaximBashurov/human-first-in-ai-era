# Information Overload (IO)

Whenever you go online with your idea, project or content, you find that "that guy" (1K of "guys", or worse - a number of cool teams, companies, corporations) have already produced an enormous amount of content of this type, spent years on that, have a huge audience of 100K+ attentive followers and in addition to that:

* 100 thematic apps and portals are available for use;
* 1K of books are written on this topic (300 pages min) that you will need half of your life to read;
* 10K of high-professional videos are there on YouTube, made with the professional equipment in fancy studios;
* 100K of comments expressing all possible attitudes accompany all that.

!!! warning "And thus, what a heck should I do that for?"

This completely applies to any AI-related topic in 2026.
